## Hi, I'm Abdelhak Samir Douli 👋

PhD candidate (joint PhD) at **Université de Lille, CRIStAL UMR 9189** 🇫🇷 and **Université Sétif 1, LAS** 🇩🇿.

I work on **machine learning combined with physics-based modelling (bond graphs)** for the **control and fault diagnosis of hybrid renewable energy systems**: photovoltaics, PEM fuel cells and green hydrogen.

### 🔬 Research

| Project | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [**bg-ml-mppt-pv**](https://github.com/abdelhaksamirdouli/bg-ml-mppt-pv) | Bond graph-driven, multi-module ML-MPPT for PV. Trained on 2,187 virtual modules, it generalises to unseen commercial panels. | Accepted, *Energies* (MDPI) |
| [**pv-ml-mppt**](https://github.com/abdelhaksamirdouli/pv-ml-mppt) | Model-agnostic ML-MPPT for PV, with exact Brent-optimised labels (10 M samples) | Accepted |
| [**pemfc-ml-mppt-hil**](https://github.com/abdelhaksamirdouli/pemfc-ml-mppt-hil) | ML-MPPT for PEM fuel cells, validated in **hardware-in-the-loop** on Speedgoat real-time targets | Not yet published |
| Fault diagnosis of PV modules | LFT bond graph residuals + deep learning for fault detection and isolation | In progress |
